Page 1The Week at Maine RoadManchester United
Club notes · Our own correspondent Real

Four wins from four for Busby's United

Thirteen goals scored and two against, with Pearson's three against Liverpool the high-water mark

Manchester United go into Saturday's home match with Middlesbrough with a perfect record. Grimsby were beaten 2-1 on the opening day, Chelsea 3-0 and Charlton Athletic 3-1 away, and on Wednesday Liverpool were beaten 5-0, Stan Pearson scoring three.

The side play every home game at Maine Road, shared with their neighbours, City. Old Trafford is still bomb-damaged from the war, and the Board has no fixed date for a return.

Mr. Busby is in his first full season, and the side have been led by the forwards: Rowley has scored in all four games and Mitten in three. Chelsea come to Maine Road on Wednesday.
