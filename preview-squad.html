Click a player on the squad page, or use the names under the file once a squad is open.

Ages, reputation, ability and wages come from the live v404 start of each season (dates of birth as of 29 August 1981 and 23 August 1986). Contract lengths are the game’s own assignment, not a sourced term for any named man. Form, morale, Listed and Wants a rise are the v404 system; opening words are Fair and Settled. A short run of marks can be shown as a worked example, not a transcription from Match or Shoot!.

International caps and goals are senior appearances only, cut at the save date, and only where a match-by-match or dated source was checked this round. Career tables list a complete previous spell when the league figures are sourced; a current spell is listed without a running total if that total would include later seasons. Personality is left as not sourced for every real player. Nationality in 1981 is the live v404 nat field; in 1986 the live squads have no nat, so this preview adds a sourced nationality for the United men only. Gaps are marked, not filled.
