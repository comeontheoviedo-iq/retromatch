RetroMatch: era commentary preview

PREVIEW This is not in the game. Three full matches, each taken from a real run of the game's match simulation on a real save, then told in the voice of the period. Every line on the left is chosen from what the sim's event log (right) says happened: the same scorers, the same minutes, the same saves, misses and corners. Nothing is invented or re-rolled. The style is evoked, not quoted from any real commentator.

Era rules respected: charging the keeper still lawful in 1946; floodlights only for a real midweek fixture after Feb 1956; one injury-only substitute in 1966-67; no yellow or red cards before 1970 (the referee only takes a name).
