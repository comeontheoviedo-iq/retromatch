This Week at Turf Moor

Burnley F.C.
Cover art · placeholder Top of the League!
Burnley's flying startTwo games, two wins, eight goals

Club notes · by our Burnley correspondent Real

Potts's Clarets start with two wins

A 3-2 win at Leeds on the opening Saturday, then a 5-2 win over Everton at Turf Moor on Tuesday night, and Burnley lead the First Division.

Pilkington, Connelly and Pointer scored at Elland Road, and on Tuesday Connelly hit two, with Pilkington, Pointer and Robson also scoring. West Ham United are the visitors on Saturday afternoon.

Where they stand

Demo table. Every score except Burnley's two is made up. Burnley's row is built from their two real results, 3-2 at Leeds and 5-2 against Everton, and the two real defeats are on those clubs' lines. The bottom two clubs go down.

How it works

Two points for a win, one for a draw. If clubs are level, the better goal average (goals for divided by goals against) goes above. The bottom two are relegated and the top Second Division pair come up.
