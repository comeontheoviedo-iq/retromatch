Notes from the Club

The directors extend a warm welcome to Northgate Rovers, their officials and supporters, and thank our own followers for their continued patience at the turnstiles, given the times. Queues are long; the Board ask that those with the right money kindly tender it.

The Secretary reminds supporters that tickets for the next home match against Haddenham Albion are on sale from the Club office, Ironworks Lane, between 10 a.m. and 12 noon, Mondays to Fridays. Season-ticket holders are asked to take their places early.

Trainer's report

The trainer reports the side fit save for one knock to the left-half. The reserves meet Northgate Rovers "A" at the Recreation Ground, kick-off 2.30.

The Board ask that this programme be passed to a friend when you have done with it. Paper is short.

    Dennis Garnett

    Centre-forward · 5ft 10in · 11st 8lb · Age 26

    Born in the town and signed from the Corn Exchange Boys' Club in 1952. Top scorer last season with twenty-four. Pleasant off the field, he likes a game of snooker and says the secret is "shooting early".

    5

    Les Ogden

    Centre-half · 6ft 0in · 12st 9lb · Age 29

    Club captain since 1957. Joined from Fenwick Albion for a modest fee and has not missed a match in two seasons. Once played an entire game with a broken nose.

    7

    Alan Fairbrother

    Outside-right · 5ft 6in · 10st 2lb · Age 22

    Quick on the wing and quicker on the bus. A local lad from the ground staff, he made his debut in the Easter fixture and hasn't looked back.
